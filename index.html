QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is focus_ 100% offline?

Offline-first means your data is completely private. There are no server crashes, zero network latency, and you can focus anywhere—whether you're on a plane or have disabled Wi-Fi to avoid notifications.

Why a flat $9 fee instead of a monthly subscription?

We believe you should own your software, not rent it. A one-time payment of $9 gives you lifetime access. Since we store all your data locally, we have no ongoing server hosting costs to charge you for.

How do I backup my goals and tasks?

All data is stored inside your phone's secure sandbox using localStorage. We don't sync data to cloud servers. If you want to backup your data, standard phone backups (Google Drive backups on Android) will automatically save your app state.

How does the profile picture upload work offline?

We've built a native bridge to your device's photo gallery. When you upload a picture, the app uses an offscreen canvas to crop and compress it to a tiny 200x200px Base64 string (~10KB). This is saved locally, meaning no files are ever sent to an external server.
